Per-phase limits from the inverter datasheet

Copy these from the datasheet (look for “max. output power per phase”, “unbalanced output” and “peak power, off-grid”). Leave the imbalance blank if the datasheet allows full (100 %) unbalanced output up to the per-phase limit. Hourly loads are compared with the continuous per-phase rating; the peak value is used for motor starting.
